Sasnaka Sansada - Learn Steer 2.0

The Vision & Mission

Sasnaka Sansada is leveling up. As we move into Learn Steer 2.0, we are embarking on a massive Digital Transformation to revolutionize the Sri Lankan education sector. We aren't just building a website; we are architecting a comprehensive educational ecosystem designed to scale impact nationwide.

We are looking for passionate Software Developers, Web Developers, and Data Enthusiasts to join our core technical team. This is a unique opportunity to work in an industry-standard environment alongside:

Undergraduates from leading State and Private universities.
Trainee & Associate Developers currently working in multinational tech firms.
Experts providing mentorship in modern tech stacks and agile methodologies.
